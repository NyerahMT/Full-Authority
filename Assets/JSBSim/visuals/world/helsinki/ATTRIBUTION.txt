Full Authority Stage 024 benchmark map

Source: Helsinki 3D reality mesh (2017), City of Helsinki
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
Source: https://3d.hel.ninja/mesh/

The source OBJ reality mesh was recentered, rotated, merged into 2 km chunks,
texture-atlased and selectively downsampled for mobile rendering. No generated
buildings, roads, vegetation, vehicles, shoreline or terrain geometry replaces
the City of Helsinki photogrammetry in the visual benchmark layer. Cells outside
the published Helsinki mesh coverage are recorded in the manifest and omitted.
