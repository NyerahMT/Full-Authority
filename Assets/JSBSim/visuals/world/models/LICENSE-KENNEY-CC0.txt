Full Authority uses a deliberately small geometry subset from two Kenney asset packs:

Kenney Nature Kit
https://kenney.nl/assets/nature-kit
Mirror/download source used for the development import:
https://opengameart.org/content/nature-kit

Kenney City Kit (Industrial)
https://kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-industrial
Mirror/download source used for the development import:
https://opengameart.org/content/city-kit-industrial

Both packs are released under Creative Commons CC0 1.0 / public domain dedication.
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Full Authority imports geometry only from the selected OBJ files and supplies its own restrained RealityKit materials. Original Kenney color textures are intentionally not shipped.
